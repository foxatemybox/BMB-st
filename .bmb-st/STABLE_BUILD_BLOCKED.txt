BMB-st stable packaging is intentionally incomplete in Git.

Direct stable finalization is expected to fail with:
BMB_ST_LAUNCHER_PREP_REQUIRED

BADLOOM Manga Launcher creates .bmb-st/launcher_prepared.json only in its local BMB-st checkout, finalizes the candidate, removes the marker, then publishes the candidate and receipt.
The marker is operational build state and must never be committed.
